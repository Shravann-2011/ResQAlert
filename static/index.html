How It Works?

ResQAlert integrates data from satellites, weather stations to create a comprehensive picture of potential disaster risks. Our AI models process this information in real-time, identifying patterns that indicate imminent threats.

When a potential disaster is detected, our system calculates impact zones, severity levels, and optimal evacuation strategies, then immediately alerts affected communities and emergency response teams.
